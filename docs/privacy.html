Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 26, 2026

DevJournal ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

DevJournal does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All data remains on your device:

  • Feed subscriptions — The RSS feeds you follow are stored locally.
  • Bookmarks — Articles you save are stored only on your device.
  • Reading history — Which articles you have read is stored locally.
  • App preferences — Theme, compact mode, and other settings are stored locally.

No Account Required

DevJournal does not require an account. There is no sign-up, login, or registration. No email, name, or other personal identifier is collected.

No Analytics or Tracking

We do not use analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, or third-party services that collect usage data. We have no access to how you use the app.

Network Requests

The app makes network requests only to fetch RSS feeds from the sources you subscribe to, and to download feed icons. No data is sent to our servers.

Third-Party Services

DevJournal does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.

Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, its security depends on your device's built-in protections (e.g., passcode, biometric lock, encryption).

Children's Privacy

DevJournal is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact
